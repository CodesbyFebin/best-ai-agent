## Topic Cluster Mapping Rules

**Rule 1: Pillar → Cluster Linking**
- Every cluster page links to its parent pillar page with anchor: `"Best AI Agent for [topic]"`
- Every pillar page links to all 50 cluster pages with anchors from the suggested titles in the CSV

**Rule 2: Sibling Interlinking**
- Each cluster page links to 2–4 sibling topics in the same pillar (same interlinking rule as the original 10-pillar doc, just applied at 50× scale)
- Anchors: related topic titles from the CSV

**Rule 3: Cross-Pillar Canonical Direction**
- Comparison/pricing/review pages are concentrated in flagship pillars (P06, P27, P31, P37, plus the whole commercial group P34–P40)
- Avoid keyword cannibalization: a comparison page lives in ONE pillar only, not duplicated across siblings

**Rule 4: India-First Flag**
- Pillars P29, P30, P31, P32, P33 are India-specific and must include INR pricing, GST, DPDP Act references, and local examples where relevant

**Rule 5: MCP-Specific Content**
- P24–P27 are MCP-focused and must link to `src/data/ MCP` entities and evidence receipts
