## Extended Analysis: Why the Distinction Matters for Buyers

### Strategy Versus Delivery
Agentic AI is the capability strategy: how your organisation plans to use autonomous systems. AI agents are the delivery: the specific tools, workflows, and integrations you build or buy. Confusing the two leads to vague vendor requirements and unclear success metrics.

### Procurement Implications
When writing an RFP, separate capability requirements from implementation requirements. Ask vendors for agentic capabilities (planning, tool use, memory) and separately for the agent implementations you will deploy.

### Governance Implications
Agentic AI as a strategy needs policy: acceptable use, escalation, data handling, and human oversight. Each AI agent needs configuration: permissions, tools, prompts, and monitoring. Both layers need owners.

### Vendor Communication
Vendors mix the terms deliberately. Force precision: ask whether a product is an agentic capability or a specific agent instance. The answer changes pricing, implementation, and risk.

### India Context
DPDP accountability sits with the deployer, not the vendor. That means your governance must cover both the strategic capability and each deployed agent.
