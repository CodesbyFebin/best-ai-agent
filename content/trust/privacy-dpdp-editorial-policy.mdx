# Privacy and DPDP Editorial Policy

Pages on BestAIAgent.in that touch on Indian data protection law, GST, cross-border data transfer, or India-region product availability must satisfy the `CRITICAL` evidence gate. This page documents the editorial rules we apply to those pages.

## What the CRITICAL gate requires

For any claim about Indian law, regulation, certification, or compliance, the claim must cite:

- The statute or regulation (e.g. the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023).
- An official government source where available (e.g. MeitY for DPDP, the GST Council for tax rates).
- Where the law is in force, the date the relevant section came into force.
- Where the law is not yet in force, the date the implementing rules were notified.

We do not paraphrase a statute. The receipt includes the exact passage and the section / schedule locator.

## What we refuse to claim

- We do not state that a product "is DPDP-compliant" without a primary source (vendor privacy policy, audit report, or certification) that supports the claim. Vendor marketing language is not sufficient.
- We do not state a universal breach notification deadline. The DPDP Act and its draft rules specify timelines that depend on the nature of the breach and the responsible party's classification. We cite the specific rule rather than assert a single number.
- We do not state a universal penalty. The Act specifies penalty ranges that depend on the nature of the violation. We cite the section and the range.
- We do not state a universal "data principal right" — the rights depend on the lawful basis for processing and on the data fiduciary's classification.
- We do not state that a vendor "stores data in India" without a primary source. Vendor data-residency claims are re-verified at the freshness window declared on [](/trust/freshness-policy).

## What we do claim

- We state the existence and effective date of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, with a primary source citation.
- We state the GST rate applicable to software / IT services in India, with a primary source citation to the GST Council Rate Schedule.
- We summarise data principal rights at a high level, with citations to the relevant sections of the Act.
- We summarise consent requirements at a high level, with citations to the relevant sections of the Act.

When a page requires more specific advice (e.g. "what should my company do?"), the page links to a qualified legal practitioner rather than answering the question itself.

## When the rules change

When a statute, regulation, or rule referenced on this site is amended, we:

- Update the affected claim in the evidence ledger with a fresh `retrievedAt` date.
- Log the change in [](/trust/corrections).
- Re-verify every affected page on the next refresh cycle.
