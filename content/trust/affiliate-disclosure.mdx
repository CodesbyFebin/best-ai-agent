# Affiliate Disclosure and Editorial Independence

BestAIAgent.in participates in vendor affiliate programmes. This page discloses that fact, explains how affiliate relationships are walled off from editorial decisions, and tells you where to find the disclosure on each affected page.

## What is an affiliate link

An affiliate link is a tracked URL that identifies the user as having arrived at a vendor from BestAIAgent.in. When the user signs up for a paid plan, the vendor pays BestAIAgent.in a commission. Affiliate commissions do not increase the price the user pays.

## Where affiliate links appear

Affiliate links appear in three contexts:

1. Inside comparison and review pages, after the verdict section, as a clearly labelled "Sign up" or "Try free" call-to-action.
2. Inside entity profile pages, as a labelled action button.
3. Inside product round-ups, in the closing paragraph.

The disclosure is reproduced on every page that links to a commercial partner. The disclosure reads: "BestAIAgent.in may earn a commission when you sign up through this link."

## The editorial firewall

The following rules apply regardless of whether a vendor is in our affiliate programme:

- Affiliate participation does not determine inclusion. We review products that are not in our affiliate programme and refuse to review products that are.
- Affiliate participation does not determine ranking. The verdict and ordering on every comparison page are determined by the declared methodology on that page.
- Affiliate participation does not determine verdicts. We do not soften a verdict to preserve a partnership, and we do not sharpen a verdict to disqualify a non-partner.
- Affiliate participation does not determine freshness. We re-verify claims on the same schedule regardless of whether the vendor is a partner.

When a vendor relationship changes (e.g. we join or leave a partner programme), we log the change in [](/trust/corrections).

## What we never do

- We never mark up a page with `Affiliate` or commercial schema to influence search rankings.
- We never swap a verdict or a recommended plan to favour a higher-commission tier.
- We never hide the disclosure behind a fold or a tooltip. The disclosure is visible to every user.

## What we always do

- We always declare the methodology on every comparison page (see [](/trust/comparison-methodology)).
- We always declare the evidence window on every review and pricing page (see [](/trust/evidence-methodology)).
- We always identify the reviewer on every page (see [](/trust/author-reviewer-policy)).
