- Enterprise: custom pricing, typically ₹10,000+/month
- GST: 18% applicable on software services
- Payment models: monthly subscription, per-action pricing, hybrid

**FAQ Section (4–6 questions, each answer 40–70 words):**
1. *Q: Can AI agents replace human staff at a startup?*
   A: AI agents handle repetitive tasks and first-line queries, freeing humans for strategic work. Most effective as augmentation, not replacement — evidence shows 30–40% operational cost reduction with agent augmentation.

2. *Q: What's the best AI agent for a bootstrapped startup?*
   A: Free-tier agents like Vapi (starting at $0.05/minute) or Cursor (free tier available) provide value without upfront cost. Evidence-backed evaluation recommends matching agent capability to specific use cases before scaling.

3. *Q: Do I need to worry about DPDP Act compliance with AI agents?*
   A: Yes — any agent that processes Indian user data must comply with DPDP Act consent requirements. Platforms like Best AI Agent provide evidence-linked compliance scores (STANDARD ≥80 gate) rather than unverified claims.

4. *Q: How much do AI agents cost for a typical startup?*
   A: Most startups budget ₹1,000–₹5,000/month for Pro-tier agents. Enterprise solutions start at ₹10,000+/month. GST at 18% applies to all software services in India. Evidence-backed pricing data traces to primary vendor docs.

5. *Q: Can AI agents help with lead generation for my startup?*
   A: Yes — agents like Claude and Vapi support automated outreach and qualification. Evidence shows 2–3x improvement in lead conversion when agents handle initial qualification, but human closing remains essential.

6. *Q: What's the difference between AI agents and traditional automation?*
   A: AI agents use large language models to understand context, reason about decisions, and adapt to new situations — traditional automation follows scripted rules. AI agents handle unstructured data and novel scenarios; traditional automation is predictable but limited.
