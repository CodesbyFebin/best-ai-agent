# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) — adapt the wording to this repository's channels.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a **harassment-free experience for everyone**, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community — and to value **evidence and accuracy** over persuasion or volume.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Demonstrating empathy, kindness, and patience.
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and accepting **evidence-grounded** feedback gracefully.
- Accepting responsibility, apologizing to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from the experience.
- Focusing on what is best for the overall community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information (physical address, email, phone), or security vulnerabilities — **vulnerabilities belong in the private channel** (see [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md)).
- Fabricated evidence: presenting claims, benchmarks, or "verified" badges without a real source receipt is treated as misconduct here, because it undermines the project's core value.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, review comments, commits, and any other public communication tied to this repository — and also applies when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement guidelines

Community leaders will follow these Community Impact Guidelines in determining the consequences for any action they deem in violation of this Code of Conduct:

1. **Correction** — for a first, minor violation. A private message explaining why the behavior was inappropriate.
2. **Warning** — for a sustained minor violation. A warning with consequences for continued behavior.
3. **Temporary ban** — for a serious violation. A temporary ban from interaction for a specified period.
4. **Permanent ban** — for a sustained pattern of serious violations, or harassment of an individual.
