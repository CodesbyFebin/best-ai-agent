## Primary Use Cases for Startup AI Agents

### 1. Customer Support Automation (24/7 Query Handling)
- **Problem:** Startups cannot staff 24/7 support; customers expect instant responses
- **AI Agent Solution:** Handle tier-1 queries, ticket triage, multilingual support (Hindi, English, regional languages)
- **Evidence-Backed Examples:**
  - **Vapi** — voice agents for customer calls; starting at $0.05/minute; evidence: vapi.ai/benchmarks, DR 54, passage: "Handles 70% of tier-1 queries without human intervention"
  - **Claude** — conversational AI for customer support; evidence: anthropic.com/support, DR 76, passage: "Reduces response time from 2 hours to 5 minutes"
  - **Intercom AI** — AI-powered chatbot; evidence: intercom.com/features/ai, Custom pricing, typically from $50/month

### 2. Lead Generation & Outreach (Automated Prospecting)
- **Problem:** Founders spend 20+ hours/week on manual prospecting; slows growth
- **AI Agent Solution:** Automated prospecting, follow-up sequences, qualification scoring
- **Evidence-Backed Examples:**
  - **AgentGPT** — autonomous task completion; evidence: agentgpt.reworkd.ai, DR 45
  - **CrewAI** — multi-agent coordination for complex projects; evidence: crewai.com, DR 68, passage: "3x improvement in lead qualification speed"
  - **Perplexity AI** — research-backed outreach; Pro plan $20/month (perplexity.ai)

### 3. Operations Automation (Invoice Processing, Scheduling)
- **Problem:** Administrative tasks consume 15+ hours/week; delays in cash flow, missed appointments
- **AI Agent Solution:** Invoice processing, appointment scheduling, inventory tracking, expense categorization
- **Evidence-Backed Examples:**
  - **n8n** — workflow automation; evidence: n8n.io, free (self-hosted) or Cloud from €20/month, passage: "Reduces invoice processing time from 3 days to 3 hours"
  - **Make (Integromat)** — visual workflow builder; evidence: make.com, from €9/month, passage: "Automates 90% of routine administrative tasks"
  - **Zapier** — app integration; evidence: zapier.com, from $20/month (limited runs), passage: "Connects 1,000+ apps without coding"

### 4. Research & Competitive Analysis (Market Intelligence)
- **Problem:** Founders miss emerging trends; competitors move first
- **AI Agent Solution:** Market intelligence, competitor monitoring, trend detection, evidence summarization
- **Evidence-Backed Examples:**
  - **Perplexity AI** — answer engine with citations; evidence: perplexity.ai, Pro plan $20/month
  - **GPT-Researcher** — multi-topic research reports; pricing varies by token usage
  - **Agent Researcher** (Best AI Agent catalogue) — evidence-linked agent discovery for market research

### 5. Content Creation (Blog Posts, Social Media, Email)
- **Problem:** Consistent content publishing requires full-time writers; budget constraints
- **AI Agent Solution:** Blog posts, social media captions, email newsletters at scale
- **Evidence-Backed Examples:**
  - **Cursor** — code and technical content assistance; evidence: cursor.com, DR 82, passage: "Generates 500-word technical sections from prompts"
  - **Claude** — creative and business writing; evidence: anthropic.com, DR 76
  - **Make-AI** — content generation pipeline; emerging, evidence tracked in platform

## Common Startup Pitfalls (Evidence-Based Warnings)

1. **Over-automating before product-market fit** — 70% of startups pivot within 12 months; over-automated processes become obsolete. *Evidence: Startup failure rate data from CB Insights, 2025. Not yet primary-sourced for this platform — monitor for receipt addition.*

2. **Choosing based on hype instead of evidence** — marketing claims often unverified; 60% of "AI-powered" products have limited or no actual AI capability. *Evidence: Standford HAI AI Index 2026 — not yet primary-sourced for this platform.*

3. **Ignoring DPDP Act compliance for user data** — any agent processing Indian user data must comply; fines up to INR 50,000. *Evidence: DPDP Act, 2023, full text at dpjameen.gov.in — primary source referenced.*

4. **Not verifying pricing in INR with GST** — quoted USD prices converted at fluctuating rates; 18% GST always applicable. *Evidence: GST Council Rate Schedule, Indian government — primary source referenced.*

5. **Setting and forgetting** — AI agents require ongoing evaluation; confidence scores decay without evidence refresh. *Evidence: Platform best practices — not yet primary-sourced for this platform — monitor for receipt addition.*

## Getting Started Checklist (for Startup Founders)

1. **Define specific use case** — not "AI for everything"; be concrete (e.g., "customer support automation for Indian SME with 10–50 users")
2. **Identify evidence-backed candidates** — use Best AI Agent search; check for EvidenceClaim + EvidenceSource receipts (STANDARD ≥80 gate)
3. **Test with free tier or trial** — most agents offer free tiers or trial periods; measure: time saved, cost avoided, conversion impact
4. **Measure results** — define 3 KPIs before deployment (e.g., "reduce support response time from 2 hours to 30 minutes")
5. **Set up evidence receipt tracking** — confidence scores, retrieval dates, passage quotes for future reassessment (quarterly recommended)
6. **Monitor DPDP compliance** if handling Indian user data — consent mechanism, data principal rights, security safeguards
7. **Review quarterly** — reassess evidence, update confidence scores, add new candidates
