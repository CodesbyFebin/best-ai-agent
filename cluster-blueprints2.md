**FAQ Section (4–6 questions, each answer 40–70 words):**
1. *Q: Does the DPDP Act apply to all AI agents in India?*
   A: Yes — any AI agent that processes personal data of Indian data principals must comply, regardless of where the agent is hosted. Exemptions exist for certain startup categories under specified thresholds.

2. *Q: What consent is required before an AI agent collects user data?*
   A: Explicit, informed, and specific consent for the stated purpose, with a practical mechanism to withdraw consent at any time. Pre-ticked boxes or assumed consent are not compliant.

3. *Q: What are the penalties for DPDP non-compliance with AI agents?*
   A: Fines up to INR 50,000 per violation, plus potential reputational damage and legal liability. Repeated violations may attract higher penalties. Evidence-backed risk assessment recommended.

4. *Q: Can AI agents use user data for model training under DPDP?*
   A: Only with explicit consent from the data principal. Anonymized data may be used without consent if true anonymization is achieved — but "pseudonymized" data still requires consent.

5. *Q: How do I verify an AI agent's DPDP compliance claims?*
   A: Evidence-backed platforms provide confidence scores (EVIDENCE_RULES: STANDARD ≥80 = 1+ primary source) tracing to primary vendor privacy policies, not marketing claims. Always check for quoted passages from actual policy documents.

6. *Q: Is GST applicable on DPDP-compliant AI agent services?*
   A: Yes — 18% GST applies to all AI agent services in India, including those designed for DPDP compliance. Input tax credit may be available for business purchasers.
